## SymphoServe — hospitality, with a human touch

SymphoServe shares the parent typography and structure while using its own forest-and-mint palette.

The shipped product (website, admin portal, product owner dashboard, kitchen display) follows the *SymphoServe UI* section and its `serve-web-`, `serve-app-`, `serve-status-` and `serve-kds-` tokens. The brand-book palette below (`serve-forest`, `serve-mint` and the rest) does not yet match that implementation — see *Decision record*.

- Surfaces: `serve-forest` for heroes and dark panels, `serve-cream` for warm light surfaces.
- Text: `serve-cream` or `white` on `serve-forest`; `charcoal` on `serve-cream`.
- Primary action on forest: `serve-mint` fill, `serve-forest` label (8.41:1), `radius-sm`, `control-height`. Example: "Request a demo".
- `serve-green` for secondary fills with `white` text (4.87:1); `serve-gold` for fine decorative detail on forest only.
- Headlines stay in Playfair Display: "Restaurant technology, made more human."
- **Symba** is the SymphoServe lion mascot (Assets → SymphoServe). Use this spelling — the earlier name SymphoSimba is retired. Preserve the supplied appearance; use Symba only in SymphoServe contexts.
